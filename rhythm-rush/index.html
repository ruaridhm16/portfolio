Rhythm Rush Simulator

GitHub

A concept for my human-centred design project - turning the boring part of a night out, queuing, into part of the fun. Up to four people in the queue compete side by side in a dance battle on a big screen, scored on rhythm and creativity.

Body tracking is used to make that possible: a camera reads each player's pose in real time and replicates it on screen. This is a rebuild of the tracking, running MediaPipe pose detection in your browser.

Works best in good lighting, with your full body in frame.
